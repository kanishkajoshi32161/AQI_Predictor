About AeroSense AI

An individual Machine Learning project created for the College Machine Learning Exhibition.

Project Objective

Urban air pollution is a critical public health threat causing millions of preventable respiratory and cardiovascular cases worldwide. Traditional physical sensor monitoring stations require expensive hardware ($10,000+) and suffer from maintenance delays.

AeroSense AI demonstrates how supervised Machine Learning regression algorithms can accurately estimate Air Quality Index (AQI) using readily available, cost-effective meteorological features (temperature, relative humidity, time of day, season, wind speed, pressure).

Scientific Methodology

Atmospheric pollutant dispersion is governed by fluid dynamics and boundary layer thermodynamics. Cold winter temperatures create thermal inversions where a layer of warm air traps cooler polluted air near the ground.

Our trained Random Forest Regressor captures these multi-dimensional interactions with an R² score of 0.940, significantly outperforming traditional linear models which assume linear proportionality.

Educational & Research Disclaimer: Predictions and estimated concentrations generated by AeroSense AI are machine-learning statistical estimates intended for educational, demonstration, and research purposes and should not replace official government air-quality monitoring stations or medical directives.
